About The Event

TechFest 2026 is an exciting technology festival organized by the Department of Information and Communication Technology. The event will bring together students, teachers, researchers and technology enthusiasts.

Students will have the opportunity to participate in programming contests, project exhibitions, robotics competitions and technology seminars.

The festival aims to encourage innovation, creativity and practical learning among university students.
