University Hosts Annual Technology Festival

Bangladesh University of Professionals recently organized its annual technology festival. Students from different departments participated in various competitions and exhibitions.

The event provided students with an opportunity to present their innovative projects and demonstrate their technical skills. Faculty members and guests also attended the event.

The university authorities appreciated the efforts of the students and encouraged them to participate in more technology-based activities.
